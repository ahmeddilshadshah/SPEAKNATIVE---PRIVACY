How Speak Native works

A quick tour of every screen, in the order you'll actually use them — from your first launch to your hundredth practice session.

Getting started Home Learning a word Vocabulary Phrases Search Practice Recording Settings Levels & Bonus
01
Getting started

Set up in under a minute

No account, no sign-up. Three quick steps and you're on Home.

Choose your base language — the language you'll read meanings and menus in. Speak Native guesses it from your device automatically.
Pick the language(s) you want to learn, in order of priority — tap them in the order you want, and each gets a number so you can see your ranking.
A 30-second orientation covers Levels, tapping to hear words, and the Practice tab — then you land on Home.
02
Home

Your Continue Learning carousel

Home always picks up exactly where you left off — for whichever language is currently active.

Switch languages any time from the picker at the top — each language keeps its own separate progress.
Core Course Progress tracks Levels 1–5; tap any Level dot to jump straight to it in the carousel.
Swipe through cards one word or phrase at a time, always starting from the next thing you haven't learned yet.
03
Learning a word

Tap to hear, mark to remember

Every card shows the native text, a pronunciation guide written for how you read, and the meaning — all at once.

Tap the card to hear it spoken aloud, with the pronunciation guide highlighting syllable by syllable as it plays.
Star to save it to Favorites, or tap the checkmark once you've got it — that's what moves the carousel forward.
Mic icon lets you record your own attempt right there, without leaving the card.
04
Vocabulary

Browse by topic, not just by order

Every word you're learning, grouped by category, with its own progress bar.

Filter by Level or category, or search across native text, pronunciation, and meaning at once.
Hide learned to see only what's left in a category.
Tap the lightning icon on any category header to jump straight into practicing just that category.
05
Phrases

Real situations, real sentences

Phrases are grouped by situation — Restaurant, Airport, Emergency, Meeting People, and more.

Each phrase shows the individual words it's built from, and whether you've learned each one yet — a gentle nudge, never a lock.
Longer phrases include a word-by-word breakdown underneath, so you can see how the native word order actually works.
06
Search

Find anything, from anywhere

One search box across your entire Vocabulary and Phrases library.

Matches the native script, the pronunciation guide, or the meaning — whichever you remember.
07
Practice

Recall it before you reveal it

The Practice tab is a folder per language — every language you've learned anything in shows up there.

You see the meaning first. Try to recall the word, then tap Reveal to check yourself.
Rate it Got it to build a streak, or Still shaky to send it back to Home's main queue for more exposure.
Jump into practicing just one category or situation directly from Vocabulary or Phrases instead of the whole language.
08
Recording

Hear yourself, next to the reference

Tap the mic to record your own pronunciation attempt, on the learning card or in Practice.

Tap once to start, tap again to stop — built for full sentences, not just quick words.
In Practice, your learning attempt and practice attempt both stay available side by side, so you can compare how you sound now versus when you first learned it.
Recordings never leave your device — see the Privacy Policy for details.
09
Settings

Make it yours

A name and photo for your Home-screen greeting, and the one setting that shapes everything else.

Your base language controls the app's own menus and which language every pronunciation guide is written for — switch it any time.
A short note in Settings explains that pronunciation guides and translations are machine-generated and not yet all native-reviewed.
10
Levels & Bonus

Nothing is ever locked

Content is organized into five Core Levels, plus an open-ended Bonus tier — jump around freely at any point.

Levels 1–5 are the Core Course — what the progress bar on Home tracks.
Level 6, Bonus, keeps going after — extra vocabulary and phrases for when you want more, marked with a small badge wherever it appears.
Tip: finishing every Core item shows a banner on Home before the carousel moves into Bonus content — that's your sign you've completed the Core Course.
